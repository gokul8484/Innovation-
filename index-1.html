How it works

Image upload → preprocessing → OCR (Tesseract) → digital text → save / download. For exam sheets, answers are compared with the teacher's key using TF-IDF cosine similarity plus keyword matching, then the teacher reviews and approves the marks.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Notes are stored in this browser's local storage.
